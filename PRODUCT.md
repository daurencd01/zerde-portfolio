# Zerde Kanat portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are schools and employers evaluating Zerde Kanat as a prospective chemistry and biology teacher.

## Product Purpose

Present Zerde's education, teaching practice, skills, languages, certificate, and contact options in Russian and Kazakh. A visitor should be able to understand her teaching approach, review evidence of classroom experience, download a CV, and contact her.

## Operating Context

The site is a public, single-page React/Vite portfolio. Visitors may browse on desktop or mobile. The current site includes two school practice entries and a Turkish B1 certificate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the Russian and Kazakh versions and current contact destinations.
- Keep the existing React/Vite stack.
- Use the published information as the source for a short CV PDF; the user confirmed there is no existing CV file.
- Lesson topics, specific materials, and measured outcomes for the two practices are undecided until supplied by the user. Do not invent them.
- The user supplied a university profile screenshot showing GPA 3.12. Publish only the GPA from that screenshot; keep its student ID and group out of the site and CV.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Zerde Kanat / Зерде Канат / Зерде Қанат.
- The user selected a scientific-journal visual direction for the redesign.
